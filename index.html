How the live queue works

Save a match's final score to free that court. Its four players rejoin the waiting pool, and the next four eligible players can be assigned immediately—even while other courts are still playing. Longest waiting times have priority. When waits are close, skill balance and previous pairings help choose the group and teams.

Session settings

Changing the court count doesn't end a match already in progress. You cannot reduce it below an occupied court number.

Queue fairness

The four longest-waiting players are the starting priority. Players with nearly equal waits (within 60 seconds of the fourth person) may be swapped to reduce mismatches. Players with substantially longer waits cannot be skipped. Teams are balanced by skill and repeated partners are penalized.
